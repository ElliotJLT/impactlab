## The framing

> This month, Claude Communities around the world are tackling AI's hard questions. The Conversation is complete. Now we act.
>
> **"What is AI doing to us?"**
>
> On 16 July, we assembled an array of normal people to talk through this subject in small groups. More about humans than AI.
>
> At the Impact Lab, we gather teams to build things that focus on the areas they identified:

### 1. "How can AI make its users better?"

> A better person, more educated, more informed, more critical. Not just at work, in the world.

### 2. "How do we make AI accessible for all?"

> Bring the benefits to non-technical backgrounds, those without computers, younger people or different cognitive needs?

### 3. "How can AI help communities thrive?"

> Instead of centralising opportunities, how can it expose them locally and match people to what's needed?

## Reading the brief

Two things worth noticing, since they shape what scores well:

**The prompts came from non-technical people, not from Anthropic.** They're about what AI does *to humans* — that's the phrase in the source conversation. A build that makes a human visibly better off will read stronger than one that shows off a technique.

**"Now we act" is the ask.** The conversation already happened. They want something built, not another analysis of the problem.
